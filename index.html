Help & primer design rules

20.1 Quick reference

GC clamp: ending a primer in G or C stabilizes the 3' end and improves polymerase extension.

Tm (melting temperature): calculated here with nearest-neighbor thermodynamics (unified SantaLucia parameters) plus salt correction — more accurate than the Wallace rule, which we fall back to only as a sanity check for very short primers.

Homopolymer runs: long stretches of one base (e.g. AAAAA) promote mispriming and slippage — kept short by the max-homopolymer filter.
